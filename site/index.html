Slop Filter

Your YouTube feed, without the AI slop.

Slop Filter hides or labels AI-generated videos on YouTube: home, search, the sidebar and Shorts. It runs entirely in your browser.

How it works

YouTube's own labelVideos YouTube marks as “altered or synthetic content” are caught automatically.
Your flagsHover any video and click “Flag as AI”. Every video from that channel disappears.
Smart rulesTitles like “AI generated” or “made with Sora” get an “AI?” label. Videos about AI, and titles that say “no AI”, are left alone.
Community list (Pro)Channels reported by 3+ people and reviewed by a human.

Nothing disappears without telling you

Unsure matches only get a label. When videos are hidden, a small note at the bottom of the page says how many, with a “Show” button. Hover any label to see why it was flagged, and click “Not AI” to never flag that channel again.
