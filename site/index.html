How it works

YouTube's own labelVideos YouTube marks as “altered or synthetic content” are hidden.
Your flagsHover any video and click “Flag AI”. That channel is gone for good.
Smart rulesTitles that say “AI generated”, “made with Sora” and similar get an “AI?” badge.
Community list (Pro)Channels reported by 3+ people and reviewed by a human.

Never hides by mistake without telling you

Unsure matches get a badge, not hidden. Every hidden video has a “Show” button and a “Not AI” button, and hovering the badge shows why it was flagged.
