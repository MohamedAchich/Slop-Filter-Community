Slop Filter

Privacy policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

Short version

Slop Filter does not collect, sell or share any personal data. Everything happens in your browser.

What stays in your browser

  • Your settings, the channels you flag or mark “Not AI”, and daily counters, saved in your browser's extension storage.
  • The extension reads video titles, channel names and labels on youtube.com pages to decide what to hide. This never leaves your browser.

Network requests (Pro only)

The free version makes no network requests. If you activate Pro, and only after you grant permission:

  • Your license key is checked with Dodo Payments (our payment provider) once a day.
  • The shared list of AI channels is downloaded from GitHub Pages twice a day. Nothing about you is sent.

Reporting a channel

“Report” opens a pre-filled GitHub issue in a new tab. Nothing is sent unless you submit it yourself with your GitHub account. Reports are public.

Payments

Payments are handled by Dodo Payments as merchant of record. We never see your card details. See Dodo Payments' privacy policy.
